Good to know

Questions, answered.

Something else? Call the shop at (555) 555-0142.

How much is a haircut?

A classic cut is $32, with the hot towel and straight-razor finish included. Kids under 12 are $22, and students, seniors and veterans get $5 off.

Do you take walk-ins?

The chair stays busy, so booking ahead is the surest way in. For a same-day opening, check the booking page or call the shop.

How do I book?

Tap Book your cut. It opens the booking page, where you pick a time and add your contact details. Rather talk to someone? Call (555) 555-0142.

Can I bring my kids?

Yes. Kids under 12 are $22, and you can book back-to-back slots, one for each of you.

Do you do shaves or beards?

Every cut finishes with a hot towel and a straight-razor shave at the neck. For your beard, ask for a quick touch-up if there’s time in your slot.

When are you open?

Tuesday to Saturday, 9 AM to 6 PM. Closed Sunday and Monday.
