Kiru:Path Not Taken Cast選ばなかった道

The people of Path Not Taken, the game where Kiru learns when not to cut. All 24 of them drawn as ninja on Kiru's own rig, each introduced as you first meet them, with nothing given away.

The story so far

Years after the old war, Kiru lives in Lord Noboru's house, the student of a master who is gone. In a world where every ninja is taught to kill, Hideo taught him to defend first. The game is about what Kiru does with that.

Kiru, the master who raised him, his best friend, and his best friend’s little brother.

Kiru

キル · 切る

Hideo’s student · Noboru’s ward

Raised in Lord Noboru’s house and trained by Master Hideo. Not the most gifted fighter in the house, just the hardest working. His name means “to cut”, and his master taught him to defend first.

H 1.00 Kiru

Master Hideo Mori

モリ・ヒデオ

Kiru’s master

The master who raised Kiru and Koji. He is gone before the story begins, and he left them one rule: defend first.

H 1.00 Kiru

Koji Hayashi

ハヤシ・コウジ

Kiru’s oldest friend

Hideo’s other student, a natural prodigy. He has sparred with Kiru for years and still tells him he leads with his shoulder.

H 1.02 Kiru

Shin Hayashi

ハヤシ・シン

Koji’s little brother

Koji’s younger brother. Growing up next to a prodigy is hard, and Shin is tired of being compared.

H 0.95 Kiru

House Sagaraサガラ家

The house that took Kiru in. Violet silk, gold thread and lacquer, and a gold fan crest on everything it owns.

Lord Noboru Sagara

サガラ・ノボル

Lord of the region · Kiru’s guardian

The lord who took Kiru into his house as his ward. Calm, careful and respected. Most people say he is the reason the old war ended.
