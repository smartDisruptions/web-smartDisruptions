Neo Dojo Castネオ道場の仲間たち

Kiru and eight futuristic heroes in three squads, plus six rogues, all drawn on Kiru's rig: AI robots, a test pilot, a hacker, a mech samurai, a few visitors from further out, and the villains Kiru was made to cut through. Same sticker outline, same moonlight, same big-head proportions. Each one gets a single neon of its own.

Kiru 切る · for scale
Head is the hero

Every head fills the top 55% of a 240 rig box, feet planted at y 232. The body stays small and stubby.

One sticker outline

Moonlit indigo #3d4c95, drawn under the fill so it reads as a die-cut sticker. Limbs are two strokes: outline, then colour.

Light from the moon

A pale rim on the upper left, a soft shade on the lower right, one faint gloss on the crown. Nothing else.

One neon each

Every character owns a signature glow and nothing else on them shares it. Vermilion stays Kiru's.

The cast

Three squads, in billing order. Each squad can carry games of its own, and for a bigger crossover they team up. Codes run in the order the characters were drawn; heights are in Kiru units (Kiru standing is 1.00).

Rogues' gallery · 悪役

The rogues

Kiru cuts through hype, so his villains are the things he cuts through. They keep the same rig and outline, but the moon turns on them: their own neon lights them from below, their brows slant in, and every one of them has crimson eyes.
